# Active Experiment — 2026-11-09

## Question
Can practical Christmas-budget and extra-income resources lead to useful affiliate actions or a paid planning product?

## Existing path
Lead with budgeting, realistic income ideas, and holiday planning. Route only relevant finance/tool referrals with clear disclosures.

## Evidence to record
Resource completions; qualified referral clicks; attributable commissions; paid planning-product decisions.

## Operating rules
- Publishing is not distribution.
- A visit, click, save, form start, or generated checkout is not a paid customer.
- Track source, useful action, qualified commercial action, money actually received, refunds/costs, and maintenance/delivery time separately where applicable.
- Use verified affiliate or booking destinations and disclose commercial relationships.
- Review on a defined cadence and choose **continue / change / pause / stop**.
- Weak results do not automatically justify more content, products, or features.

## Stop rule
Do not publish high-risk finance claims or add financial products solely for commission value.

## Portfolio role
This site earns more build work by producing useful audience evidence, commercial evidence, or a clearly documented usability gap. Otherwise it stays in maintenance/distribution mode.
